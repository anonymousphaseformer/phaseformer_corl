Push-T training of the four models in Fig.~\ref{fig:pusht_pareto}, 500 epochs of Diffusion Policy's low-dimensional recipe, mean over 10 seeds (band: $\pm 1$ s.d.). \textbf{Top}: training loss. For the policy arms this is the phase-velocity MSE alone, so a score arm is comparable with the arm whose network it shares; for the chunk baselines it is their own objective (DP's denoising MSE, MIP's regression MSE, ACT's L1 + 10 KL), which is neither the arms' nor each other's. \textbf{Bottom}: the EMA model's validation loss, the quantity every arm's checkpoint is selected on; the dot marks the selected epoch. The training row is log scale, as is the baselines' validation panel; the arms' validation loss is linear. The four model columns share their axes; the baselines keep their own.
